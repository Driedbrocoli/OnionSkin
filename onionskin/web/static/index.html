  1. Put the printed sheet back in the tray. Check which way up and which end goes first.
  2. Print at 100% / “Actual size”. Turn off “Fit to page” and “Shrink oversized pages” — they scale by a few percent and nothing will line up.
  3. Do one sheet first and hold it against the original before committing the rest.
Calibrate a printer  — gets you from ±2 mm to ±0.5 mm
  1. Print the target below on blank paper at 100%.
  2. Put that same sheet back in the tray and print the target again.
  3. Each crosshair now has two impressions. Read how far the second landed from the first — right is +x, down is +y — against the printed ruler.
